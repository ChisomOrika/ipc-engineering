## TL;DR
Acquisition is working — we went from 21 to 23 active brands in March, retention is 95%. **The problem is what happens after sign-up.** Only 3 of 23 brands (Papas Grill, Wings Bistro, Citysubs) generate **82.6% of DAASH revenue**. The other 20 sit on the platform processing POS but not driving online traffic — and our service charge is tied to online activity, which is why it's stuck at ₦3M and dipped this month.

**Adding brand #24 and #25 doesn't fix anything if they end up in the same dead zone as the other 20. We need to activate the brands we already have.**

---

## The Numbers
- **23 active DAASH brands**, top 3 = 82.6% of revenue
- **POS = 81.4% of sales, website = 18.6% (flat)** — most brands use us as a POS, not an online ordering channel
- **Service charge stuck at ₦3M, declined this month**
- Same 3 brands dominate **GoSource too** (Papas Grill 39.3% share) — same concentration risk on both sides
- **But**: mid-tier brands ARE moving — Mr. Krums +52.8%, Captains Cafeteria +27.3%, Pitakwa +13.6%. Activation IS possible, we just don't have a system for it.

## Why It's Happening
Small Nigerian food businesses don't know how to drive online traffic. They don't run Google Ads, they don't optimise Instagram for orders, they don't think about digital marketing. We gave them a website and ordering page and hoped they'd bring customers. They won't. We either help them get traffic, or we build platform features that do it for them.

---

## What to Do — Three Strategic Bets

### 1. Use the GoSource × DAASH Overlap (our unfair advantage)
Papas Grill, Wings Bistro, Citysubs, Spicy Corner, Ajebo Chops are on **both** platforms. We supply their ingredients **and** process their customer orders. Nobody else in Nigeria has both sides of these businesses.

**Practical moves:**
- **Monthly one-pager with every GoSource invoice** — show each brand their DAASH metrics: online orders, trend vs last month, where they rank. Most owners don't know their online channel is stagnant. Put the data in front of them, some will act.
- **GoSource account managers become the activation channel** — they're already on the phone weekly about deliveries. "By the way, your online orders dropped 5% — want us to help?" is not cold-selling, it's an existing supplier flagging something.
- **Cross-platform commercial flywheel** — "Brands hitting 500+ DAASH online orders/month get 5% off GoSource supplies." Now they're incentivised to grow online (which grows our service charge), and the discount keeps them sticky on GoSource. Competitors can't replicate this because they don't own both sides.

### 2. Reward-Based Milestone Triggers, Not Paywalls
Right now subscription is flat — pay, get access, no progression. We're leaving upgrade revenue on the table.

- **Tier 1 (Starter)** — basics. Monthly nudge: *"You did 316 orders. Brands like yours that use Smart Menu Insights average 450. You're 84 away from unlocking it."* Specific, achievable, hooks them on growth instead of cancellation.
- **Tier 2 (unlocked at milestone, not payment)** — at 500 orders/month, brand gets *"You unlocked Smart Menu Insights for 30 days."* They didn't pay — they earned it. They taste the value. When the 30 days end, the upgrade conversation sells itself.
- **Tier 3 (Growth plan)** — AI features (win-back WhatsApp, automated campaigns, inventory intel) gated at 1,000 orders/month + payment. Status as much as price.

This is the **Shopify model** — you grow into the next plan because the math makes sense at your scale, not because someone pressured you.

### 3. Help Brands Get Traffic — 5 Things We Can Do Without Building Anything
One person dedicated to brand success could execute all five across all 23 brands in a month:
1. **Set up Google Business Profiles** for every brand pointing to their DAASH ordering page (1 week, 1 person)
2. **WhatsApp broadcast templates** — write them, give to brands; or send them on their behalf during onboarding. WhatsApp = 98% open rate vs email's 15-20%. In Nigeria this is THE channel.
3. **Instagram "Order Food" button** pointing to DAASH page (10 mins per brand)
4. **QR flyers in GoSource deliveries** — print small table cards with QR linking to their DAASH page; restaurant puts them on tables/in takeaway bags. *Owner.com (US, $178M raised) does exactly this and grows online ordering 2-4x.*
5. **Platform-wide automated holiday campaigns** — we already know Sallah = +20%, Valentine's = 2.2x. Run them centrally on WhatsApp for every brand. Brands don't lift a finger.

## AI Features — Don't Sell "AI," Sell Outcomes
Three features that would directly drive service charge revenue:

- **Win-back WhatsApp** — customer hasn't ordered in 14 days → auto WhatsApp: *"Your usual jollof from Citysubs is waiting."* Drives repeat orders → drives our service charge.
- **Smart Menu Suggestions** — *"Your grilled chicken combo outsells everything 3:1 on Fridays. Feature it Friday."* Owners don't know what their data says; show them, they value the platform.
- **GoSource × DAASH inventory intelligence** — *"You ordered 50kg chicken but only sold 30 chicken dishes — possible over-ordering."* No competitor can build this because no competitor has both supply and sales data. **This is the moat.**
